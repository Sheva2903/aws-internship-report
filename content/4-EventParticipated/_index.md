This section summarizes the events I participated in during the First Cloud AI Journey program.

The events provided opportunities to learn from real projects, listen to practical experiences, and better understand how AWS and AI technologies are applied in product development.

### [Event 1 - FCAJ Agentic AI Build Week](4.1-Event1/)

&emsp;**Date & Time:** 09:00, July 25, 2026  

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City  

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  

&emsp;**Overview:** Teams that participated in Agentic AI Build Week presented their products and shared their competition journey, including their ideas, development process, challenges, and lessons learned.

&emsp;**What I learned:** I gained a better understanding of how Agentic AI can be applied to practical products, as well as the importance of clear project scope, teamwork, testing, and product presentation.
